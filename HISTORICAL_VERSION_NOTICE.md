## Withdrawn figures

The four files named `Figure 1_ …` to `Figure 4_ …` now contain only retirement notices. The original figures (TikZ sources with colon filenames, removed in commit 09aa4fb) remain in Git history and in older external copies. All four are withdrawn, including the implied search/selection flowchart (Figure 2), which had no supporting screening records.

**Withdrawn Figure 3, "Representative Opioid Reduction Percentages Across Surgical Specialties".** This bar chart showed "opioid reduction (%)" values of roughly 60–75% labelled by specialty and first author (Beloeil, Jin, Bakan, D'Amico, McCullough). The chart gave no source data, outcome definitions, denominators, comparators or confidence intervals, and the percentages could not be traced to the cited studies. They are **unsourced and must not be cited or reused.**

The chart also mislabelled the POFA trial (Beloeil H et al., *Anesthesiology* 2021;134(4):541-551, [doi:10.1097/ALN.0000000000003725](https://doi.org/10.1097/ALN.0000000000003725)) as "Orthopedic" with a 70% reduction. POFA was a randomized trial in **major or intermediate noncardiac surgery**. Its primary composite outcome (postoperative hypoxemia, ileus or cognitive dysfunction) was **more frequent** with the dexmedetomidine opioid-free regimen (78% vs 67%), and the trial was **stopped early after five cases of severe bradycardia**. Lower postoperative opioid use was a secondary finding, not the trial's main result. Presenting POFA as evidence for a large, beneficial opioid reduction was wrong.

The current record ([MANUSCRIPT.md](MANUSCRIPT.md), its PDF and the [supersession record on Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/23705335)) supersedes this chart and every other historical figure, efficacy percentage and method claim. Where an older copy disagrees with the current record, the current record governs.
