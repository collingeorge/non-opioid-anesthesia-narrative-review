# Non-Opioid Anesthesia for Perioperative Pain Management: A Narrative Review

**Author:** Collin B. George, BS  
**Affiliation:** University of Washington Medical Center  
**Status:** Under review for co-authorship    

## Abstract

The United States opioid epidemic necessitates safer perioperative pain management. This narrative review synthesizes evidence (2020-2024) demonstrating non-opioid anesthesia (NOA) achieves 50-80% opioid reduction while maintaining acceptable analgesia across surgical specialties.

**Key Findings:**
- Substantial opioid reduction (50-80%) with acceptable trade-offs
- Non-inferior analgesia in orthopedic and abdominal procedures  
- Integration with ERAS protocols shortens hospital stays by 1-2 days
- Comprehensive clinical protocol comparison table included
